What is it?

Selank is a synthetic peptide originally developed for anxiety and stress support. It is known for calming, anti-anxiety, and cognitive-enhancing effects without the addictive potential commonly associated with benzodiazepines.

Key Benefits

  • Helps reduce anxiety and stress
  • Supports mood and emotional balance
  • May improve focus, memory, and cognitive function
  • Supports neuroprotection and brain recovery
  • May support immune and antiviral function

Possible Side Effects

Usually rare/mild: injection-site irritation, headache, fatigue, nausea, sinus irritation with intranasal use.

Dosing Protocol

10mg vial + 2.5mL (250 units) BAC water. Subcutaneous or intranasal; best fasted when possible; lowest effective dose; cycle 4–6 weeks; washout 2–4 weeks. Intranasal has faster onset; SubQ slower/steadier.

Cognitive: 6u=250mcg or 7u=300mcg, once daily, preferably morning.
